# 6. Conclusions

In the era of general artificial intelligence, LSF-Models are revolutionizing multiple industries. However, their application in wind turbine PHM remains underexplored. This paper reviews the literature on wind turbine PHM methodologies over the past decade, including a biblio-metric analysis. It studies key fault mechanisms in critical components and compiles 15 publicly available datasets. Traditional PHM methods, such as SVM, CNN, and LSTM, face challenges in generalization and adaptability when applied to wind turbines. In contrast, LSF-Models offer strong generalization capabilities and superior performance, making them suitable for cross-task, cross-component, and cross-turbine applications. This paper explores the use of LSF-Models in wind turbine PHM for anomaly detection, fault diagnosis, RUL prediction, and maintenance decision-making. It also discusses key technologies, such as multimodal alignment and fusion, fine-tuning techniques, integration with local knowledge bases, and intelligent agent technologies, to improve the effectiveness of LSF-Models in wind turbine PHM. LSF-Models have significant potential to enhance operational efficiency and reduce maintenance costs in wind turbines.

However, applying LSF-Models in wind turbine PHM presents several challenges, including multimodal data quality and fusion issues, privacy and security issues of wind turbine data, multi-agent collaborative issues, interpretability and trustworthiness issues of LSF-Models, computational resources and economic cost issues. Furthermore, this paper discusses future research directions, including data preprocessing and multimodal fusion, privacy protection of wind turbine data, general foundation models for wind turbine PHM, multi-agent collaborative scheduling, PHM assistant systems, and trust-aware and interpretable LSF-Models. Finally, a technical roadmap for wind turbine PHM based on LSF-models is proposed. With the continuous emergence of new technologies, wind turbine PHM based on LSF-Models is expected to advance rapidly. The literature review presented in this paper will provide a valuable reference for future research in the field of wind turbine PHM.
